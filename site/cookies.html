NEXUS-Q · Website information

Cookies and local storage

Last reviewed: 24 October 2026

The website is designed to avoid advertising and behavioural-tracking cookies. The homepage currently stores the light/dark theme choice in browser local storage (nq-theme), which is different from a cookie and is used only to remember the interface preference.

Cookies used by this site

The site does not intentionally set first-party analytics, advertising, or social-tracking cookies. No cookie-consent banner is shown because the site's current first-party theme preference uses local storage rather than a cookie and the site does not intentionally enable non-essential cookie tracking.

Third-party resources

Google Fonts and jsDelivr are used to load fonts and some SDK logo images. A browser request to a third-party resource can disclose technical information to that provider and the provider may use its own technologies under its own policies. This page does not claim that third-party providers never set cookies; their behaviour depends on their services and current configuration.

Manage your preferences

You can clear local storage and cookies for this site in your browser's site settings, or block third-party requests with browser privacy controls. Clearing nq-theme removes the saved theme preference; the site will use your system theme until you choose one again.

Review when the site changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other non-essential cookies are added later, the operator should reassess applicable consent requirements before enabling them. Where consent is required, accepting and rejecting should be offered with equal prominence and preferences should be changeable later. See the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) guidance.

This page describes the known site implementation as reviewed on 24 October 2026. It is not a substitute for testing actual browser traffic and cookies after deployment.

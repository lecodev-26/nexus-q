02 / LEARN PQC

Understand the building blocks.

Post-quantum cryptography uses mathematical problems believed to resist attacks from both classical and large-scale quantum computers. Standards matter; claims should stay precise.

FIPS 203

ML-KEM-768

Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism. It lets two parties establish a shared secret over a public channel.

Public key→Encapsulate→Shared secret
Read the NIST standard ↗
FIPS 204

ML-DSA-65

Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm. It provides public-key signatures for authenticity and integrity.

Private key→Sign→Verify

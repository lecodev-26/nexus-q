Privacy policy

Last reviewed: 24 October 2026

This policy describes the intended data practices for the public NEXUS-Q website at nexus-q.link. The site is a static project-information website and does not intentionally provide user accounts, a contact form, advertising, or first-party behavioural analytics.

Data processed when you visit

  • Server and security logs: the hosting/CDN provider may process technical request data such as IP address, request time, requested URL, user-agent, referrer, and security signals to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures. The precise fields, retention period, and controller/processor arrangements depend on the provider's current configuration and must be confirmed by the site operator.
  • Theme preference: if you select light or dark mode on the homepage, the site stores the preference in your browser's local storage under nq-theme. It is used only to remember your display choice. It is not a cookie and is not sent to the server with each request. You can clear it in your browser's site-data settings.
  • Third-party resources: the current page loads fonts from Google Fonts and some SDK logos from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to those providers to retrieve these assets; those providers may receive technical request data such as your IP address. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests. If you prefer not to make those connections, use browser protections or block the external resources.
  • Outbound links: when you choose a GitHub, package registry, or other external link, that provider receives the request and applies its own policies.

Purposes and legal basis

Technical request processing is used to deliver and secure the website and troubleshoot operational issues. The site operator should confirm the hosting provider's actual processing and document the appropriate legal basis, retention period, and processor terms before treating this summary as final. The display preference is stored locally at your request when you select a theme.

Recipients and international transfers

Hosting/CDN, Google Fonts, and jsDelivr may receive technical data when their services are used. These providers may process data in different jurisdictions under their applicable terms. The operator should verify the exact service configuration, applicable transfer safeguards, and provider agreements.

Retention

The theme preference remains in your browser until you clear it or site data is removed. Server-log retention is controlled by the hosting/security provider and the site's account configuration; the exact period has not been independently confirmed for this notice.

Your rights

Where data-protection law applies, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, and complain to a supervisory authority. Requests can be raised through the maintainer's GitHub profile or the project repository. A public issue is not suitable for private personal-data requests; do not post personal or sensitive information publicly. A dedicated private contact channel should be added by the operator.

Children

This project website is technical documentation and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This notice should be updated when the site's technologies, hosting, or processing change. The public project maintainer is lecodev-26. The operator's legal identity and a dedicated contact email have not been provided for this page and must be added if required.

Transparency note:

Some operational details—especially hosting-log retention, controller identity, contact details, and provider arrangements—must be confirmed by the operator. This page does not claim that those unverified items have already been completed.

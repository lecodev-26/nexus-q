NEXUS-Q · Website information

Privacy policy

Last reviewed: 9 November 2026

This policy describes the intended data practices for the public NEXUS-Q website at nexus-q.link. The site is a static project-information website and does not provide user accounts or a contact form. It uses Google Tag Manager (container GTM-PMTD89PQ) to load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-L9K7D4KN1E) only after a visitor affirmatively accepts analytics in the consent banner. Advertising features are not enabled.

Analytics (optional)

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 may process usage data such as page views, approximate location derived from network information, browser/device information, and interaction events. Google Tag Manager is used to deliver the analytics tag. If you reject or have not yet chosen, the analytics container is not loaded. You can change your choice using the “Privacy preferences” control shown on the site. Google processes data under its own terms and privacy documentation; review Google's policies before relying on this notice as a complete legal assessment.

NEXUS-Q information chat

If you choose to use the website chat, the message you submit is sent to a NEXUS-Q serverless endpoint hosted by Vercel so it can return a predefined response. The chat does not use an AI model, does not require an AI provider, and the application code does not intentionally store chat transcripts. The hosting provider may process technical request data in its operational logs; its retention and processing terms should be checked by the operator. Do not submit passwords, private keys, sensitive personal data, or confidential security details. If you contact the project through Telegram instead, Telegram processes your messages under its own privacy policy.

Data processed when you visit

  • Server and security logs: the hosting/CDN provider may process technical request data such as IP address, request time, requested URL, user-agent, referrer, and security signals to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures. The precise fields, retention period, and controller/processor arrangements depend on the provider's current configuration and must be confirmed by the site operator.
  • Theme preference: if you select light or dark mode on the homepage, the site stores the preference in your browser's local storage under nq-theme. It is used only to remember your display choice. It is not a cookie and is not sent to the server with each request. You can clear it in your browser's site-data settings.
  • Third-party resources: the current page loads fonts from Google Fonts and some SDK logos from jsDelivr. Your browser connects to those providers to retrieve these assets; those providers may receive technical request data such as your IP address. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests. If you prefer not to make those connections, use browser protections or block the external resources.
  • Outbound links: when you choose a GitHub, package registry, or other external link, that provider receives the request and applies its own policies.

Purposes and legal basis

Technical request processing is used to deliver and secure the website and troubleshoot operational issues. If you submit a chat question, the message is processed to return a predefined project-information response. Optional analytics is activated only after the visitor selects “Accept analytics”; rejecting analytics does not affect access to the site or the chat. The site operator should confirm the hosting provider's actual processing and document the appropriate legal basis, retention period, and processor terms before treating this summary as final. The display preference is stored locally at your request when you select a theme.

Recipients and international transfers

Hosting/CDN, Google Fonts, and jsDelivr may receive technical data when their services are used. These providers may process data in different jurisdictions under their applicable terms. The operator should verify the exact service configuration, applicable transfer safeguards, and provider agreements.

Retention

The theme preference remains in your browser until you clear it or site data is removed. Server-log retention is controlled by the hosting/security provider and the site's account configuration; the exact period has not been independently confirmed for this notice.

Your rights

Where data-protection law applies, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, and complain to a supervisory authority. Privacy requests can be sent to lecodevv@outlook.es. Do not send sensitive information through public GitHub issues.

Children

This project website is technical documentation and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This notice should be updated when the site's technologies, hosting, or processing change. The named project contact is Manuel Echepares Valderrama (lecodevv@outlook.es).

NEXUS-Q · Website information

Cookies and local storage

Last reviewed: 9 November 2026

The website stores the light/dark theme choice in browser local storage (nq-theme). It also stores your analytics choice in local storage (nq-analytics-consent). Google Analytics 4 is loaded through Google Tag Manager only after you select “Accept analytics”; it is not loaded if you reject or have not yet made a choice.

Analytics choice and cookies

The site presents equally prominent “Accept analytics” and “Reject analytics” choices. Analytics is off by default and Google Tag Manager is not loaded until consent is granted. If accepted, Google Analytics 4 may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's implementation. You can change your choice at any time with the “Privacy preferences” control at the bottom of the page. Your choice is stored locally in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Third-party resources

Google Fonts and jsDelivr are used to load fonts and some SDK logo images. A browser request to a third-party resource can disclose technical information to that provider and the provider may use its own technologies under its own policies. This page does not claim that third-party providers never set cookies; their behaviour depends on their services and current configuration.

Manage your preferences

You can clear local storage and cookies for this site in your browser's site settings, or block third-party requests with browser privacy controls. Clearing nq-theme removes the saved theme preference; the site will use your system theme until you choose one again.

Review when the site changes

If advertising, embedded media, or other non-essential technologies are added later, the operator should reassess applicable consent requirements before enabling them. Where consent is required, accepting and rejecting should be offered with equal prominence and preferences should be changeable later. See the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) guidance.

This page describes the known site implementation as reviewed on 24 October 2026. It is not a substitute for testing actual browser traffic and cookies after deployment.
